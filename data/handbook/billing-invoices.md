# Billing and invoices

## Invoice schedule
Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and cover all parcels scanned during the previous month. Payment is due within 14 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer or by card.

## Fuel surcharge
A fuel surcharge is applied as a percentage of the transport price. The percentage is revised every month and published in the portal on the 25th for the following month.

## Disputing an invoice
A billing dispute must be opened within 30 days of the invoice date, from the invoice page in the portal or by writing to billing@kestrel-parcel.example. Lines that are not disputed remain due on the normal date.

## Credit notes
An approved credit, for example for a late Express parcel, is issued as a credit note and deducted from the next invoice. Credit notes are not paid out separately unless the account is being closed.

## Paper copies
Invoices are delivered as PDF in the portal. A paper copy by post can be requested and costs 2 EUR per invoice.
