# Data privacy and retention

## Recipient data
Recipient names, addresses and phone numbers are kept for 24 months after delivery and then anonymised. Phone numbers and email addresses of recipients are used only for delivery notifications, never for marketing.

## Proof of delivery
Signatures and delivery photos are kept for 36 months, because they may be needed as evidence in a claim.

## Data requests
A person can ask for a copy of their data or for its deletion. Requests are answered within 30 days. They are handled by the data protection officer, at privacy@kestrel-parcel.example.

## Where data is stored
All customer data is stored in data centres in the European Union, in Frankfurt and Paris. Backups are encrypted and kept for 35 days.
