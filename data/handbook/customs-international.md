# International shipping and customs

## Required documents
Every parcel that leaves the customs union needs a commercial invoice in 3 copies, attached to the outside of the parcel in a document pouch. The invoice must state the value, the currency and a clear description of each item.

## HS codes and EORI
A Harmonized System (HS) code is required for each item when the goods are worth more than 150 EUR in total. Business shippers must also give their EORI number. Without it, the export declaration cannot be filed.

## Duties and taxes
By default parcels travel DAP (delivered at place): the recipient pays duties and taxes on arrival. The sender can choose DDP (delivered duty paid) instead. Kestrel then pays on the sender's behalf and invoices the amount with a handling fee of 6 EUR per parcel.

## Customs holds
A parcel stopped by customs for missing paperwork gets status code CU-301 (missing invoice). The sender has 14 days to supply the documents through the portal. After that the parcel is returned at the sender's cost.
