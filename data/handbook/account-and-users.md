# Account and user management

## Roles
There are four roles. The Owner controls everything, including bank details and closing the account. An Admin manages users and settings. A Shipper creates shipments and pickups. A Viewer can only read tracking and invoices.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication is mandatory for the Owner and for every Admin. It is optional for Shippers and Viewers. Codes come from an authenticator app; SMS codes are not offered.

## Inactive users
A user who has not signed in for 90 days is disabled automatically. An Admin can re-enable the user, who then has to choose a new password.

## Single sign-on and user limits
Single sign-on with SAML is available on the Business plan. The Starter plan allows up to 50 users. The Business plan has no user limit.
