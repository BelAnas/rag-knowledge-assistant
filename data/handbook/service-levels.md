# Service levels and transit times

## Standard service
Kestrel Standard is the default service for domestic parcels. Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days after the first depot scan. Standard parcels are delivered Monday to Friday between 08:00 and 18:00. There is no delivery-time guarantee on Standard.

## Express service
Kestrel Express delivers on the next business day before 18:00, provided the parcel is handed over before the 16:00 cut-off. Parcels handed over after the cut-off travel the following day. Express is available for domestic destinations and for the main cities of neighbouring countries.

## Economy service
Kestrel Economy is the lowest-cost option and takes 5 to 8 business days. Economy parcels travel by road only and are consolidated at regional hubs, so tracking updates are less frequent than on the other services.

## Saturday delivery
Saturday delivery is an option on Express only. It costs a surcharge of 4.50 EUR per parcel and must be selected when the label is created. It cannot be added once the parcel has been collected.

## Express guarantee
If an Express parcel arrives more than 24 hours after the promised time, the shipping fee for that parcel is credited to the account. The guarantee does not apply to delays caused by severe weather, customs holds, an incorrect address, or during the peak season.
