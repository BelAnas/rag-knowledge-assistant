# Label printing

## Label format
The shipping label measures 100 x 150 mm (4 x 6 inches). Thermal printers at 203 dpi are supported with the ZPL format. Office printers can use the PDF format on A4 paper with self-adhesive sheets.

## Barcode quality
The main barcode is a Code 128 symbol. Keep a quiet zone of 6 mm free of text and tape on both sides of it. Do not fold the label over an edge of the box and do not cover the barcode with glossy tape, which reflects the scanner light.

## Reprints
A label can be reprinted from the portal up to 5 times at no cost. Reprints keep the same tracking number.

## Label validity
A label must be used within 14 days of its creation. An older label is rejected at the first scan and a new one has to be created. Unused labels are never billed.
