# Tracking and status codes

## Tracking numbers
A Kestrel tracking number starts with the letters KS, followed by 10 digits and a two-letter country code, for example KS0048213377DE. Tracking events are kept for 180 days after delivery.

## Pickup and depot codes
PU-204 means the premises were closed at pickup. DP-220 means the parcel arrived at a depot. DP-235 means a sorting delay: the parcel missed its planned linehaul and will leave on the next one.

## Delivery codes
DL-400 means delivered. DL-417 means the recipient was absent and a new attempt is planned. AD-110 means the address is incomplete and the parcel is on hold.

## Exception codes
EX-500 means the parcel was damaged in transit and is being inspected. EX-512 means the label was unreadable and the parcel was relabelled at the depot, which usually adds one day. CU-301 means customs is waiting for an invoice.
