# Parcel limits and packaging standards

## Size and weight limits
A single parcel may weigh at most 31.5 kg. The longest side may not exceed 175 cm, and the length plus girth may not exceed 300 cm. Parcels above these limits are refused at pickup or returned from the depot.

## Volumetric weight
Light but bulky parcels are charged by volumetric weight. It is calculated as length x width x height in centimetres, divided by 5000. The invoice uses whichever is higher: the actual weight or the volumetric weight.

## Packaging requirements
Use a rigid cardboard box in good condition. Parcels heavier than 15 kg need a double-wall box. Leave at least 5 cm of cushioning on every side of the item. Seal all seams with tape that is at least 48 mm wide. Bags tied with string, and boxes strapped together, are not accepted.

## Oversize handling
A parcel that is inside the limits but cannot travel on the sorting belt, for example a tube or an irregular shape, is handled by hand. Manual handling adds an oversize surcharge of 12 EUR per parcel and may add one business day to the transit time.
