# Restricted and prohibited items

## Lithium batteries
Lithium batteries are accepted only when they are installed in the device they power, and only up to 100 Wh per battery. Loose batteries and power banks are not accepted on Express, because Express parcels may travel by air. They may travel on Standard and Economy with the battery label applied.

## Items we do not carry
Kestrel does not carry cash, bank cards, aerosols, fireworks, perishable food outside the Cool service, or live animals. Parcels that contain these items are stopped at the depot.

## Plants
Live plants are accepted on Standard, for domestic destinations only. They must be packed upright in a box marked on two sides. Plants are not eligible for damage compensation.

## Undeclared restricted goods
When a parcel is found to contain undeclared restricted goods, it is returned to the sender and a return fee of 15 EUR is charged. Repeated cases can lead to a review of the account by the compliance team.
