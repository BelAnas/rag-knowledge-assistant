# Support hours and escalation

## Opening hours
Customer support is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 19:00 and on Saturday from 09:00 to 13:00, Central European Time. Support is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Response targets
The target for a first reply is 4 business hours by email and 2 minutes in chat. These are targets, not guarantees.

## Priority 1 incidents
A Priority 1 incident is one where the customer cannot create or ship any parcel. The target for a first reply is 30 minutes. Business plan customers can call a phone line that is staffed 24 hours a day for Priority 1 incidents only.

## Escalation path
A ticket that tier 1 has not solved after 1 business day goes to tier 2. If tier 2 cannot solve it within 2 more business days, the duty manager takes ownership and calls the customer.
