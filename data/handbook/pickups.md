# Pickups

## Booking a pickup
A pickup can be booked in the portal or through the API. Bookings made before 12:00 are collected the same day. Bookings made later are collected on the next business day. There is no minimum: a pickup can be booked for one parcel.

## Pickup windows
The sender chooses one of two windows: morning, 09:00 to 13:00, or afternoon, 13:00 to 18:00. Drivers cannot commit to an exact time inside a window.

## Recurring pickups
An account that ships at least 10 parcels per week can ask for a recurring pickup on fixed days. Recurring pickups are free. They are reviewed every quarter and removed when the volume stays under the threshold for two months.

## Missed pickups
The driver waits up to 5 minutes at the collection point. If nobody is available, the stop is recorded with status code PU-204 (premises closed). The first rebooking is free. Each further rebooking for the same parcels costs 3 EUR.
