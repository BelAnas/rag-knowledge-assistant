# Peak season rules

## Peak period
The peak season runs from 15 November to 5 January. During this period volumes are about twice the normal level and several rules change.

## Changes to services
The Express guarantee is suspended during the peak season. The cut-off for same-day pickup bookings moves from 12:00 to 10:00. Recurring pickups are not affected.

## Peak surcharge
A peak surcharge of 0.60 EUR per parcel applies to all services during the peak period. It is shown as a separate line on the invoice.

## Volume forecasts
Accounts that ship more than 1,000 parcels per week must send a weekly volume forecast for the peak period by 1 October. Capacity is reserved on the basis of the forecast; volume above 120 percent of it may be collected a day later.
