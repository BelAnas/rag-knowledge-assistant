# Damage and loss claims

## Reporting damage
Damage must be reported within 7 calendar days of delivery. The claim form asks for four photos: the outer box, the shipping label, the inner packaging and the damaged item. Keep the packaging until the claim is closed, because an inspector may ask to see it.

## When a parcel counts as lost
A parcel is treated as lost when it has had no tracking scan for 10 business days. Before that, support opens a trace with the last depot that scanned it.

## Compensation
Under standard liability, compensation is 25 EUR per kilogram of actual weight, up to 500 EUR per parcel. Shipping fees are refunded when the parcel is lost. Compensation is paid as a credit note unless the customer asks for a bank transfer.

## Decision time
Claims are decided within 10 business days after all documents have been received. A claim that is refused comes with the reason, and the customer has 30 days to ask for a second review.
