# Temperature-controlled shipping (Kestrel Cool)

## Temperature range and coverage
Kestrel Cool keeps parcels between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius. It is offered for domestic destinations only. The maximum transit time is 48 hours, and a Cool parcel may weigh at most 20 kg.

## Dispatch days
Cool parcels are collected Monday to Thursday. There is no Friday collection, so that no parcel stays in the network over a weekend.

## Packing
Kestrel supplies the insulated box. Gel packs must be frozen for at least 24 hours before packing and placed above and below the goods. Do not use dry ice.

## Temperature records
Each Cool parcel travels with a data logger. The temperature readings can be downloaded from the tracking page for 30 days after delivery.

## Price
Cool adds a surcharge of 9 EUR per parcel to the Express rate.
