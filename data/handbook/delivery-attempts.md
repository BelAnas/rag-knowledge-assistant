# Delivery attempts

## Number of attempts
Kestrel makes up to 3 delivery attempts, on consecutive business days. After each failed attempt the recipient receives a notification with a link to choose another day or a parcel shop.

## Held at depot
After the third failed attempt the parcel is held at the local depot for 7 calendar days. The recipient can collect it with an identity document and the tracking number. A parcel that is not collected is returned to the sender.

## Safe place delivery
A driver may leave a parcel in a safe place only when the recipient has given consent in the Kestrel app for that address. Without consent the driver must not leave the parcel unattended.

## Signature
A signature is required for every parcel with a declared value above 500 EUR, and whenever the sender selects the signature option. The signature is captured on the driver's handheld and stored as proof of delivery.

## Delivery to a neighbour
When the recipient is absent, the driver may hand the parcel to a neighbour in the same building and leave a card. Senders who do not want this print the code NO-NBR on the label by ticking "No neighbour delivery" at label creation.
