# Address correction and redirection

## Before dispatch
An address can be corrected free of charge until the parcel receives its first scan. Open the portal, go to Shipments, select the parcel and choose Edit address.

## After dispatch
Once the parcel has been scanned, an address correction costs 3.90 EUR. The change takes effect within 2 hours. If the parcel is already on the delivery vehicle, the correction applies to the next attempt.

## What cannot be changed
A parcel cannot be redirected to another country, and the service level cannot be changed after collection. For those cases the parcel has to be returned to the sender and shipped again.

## Incomplete addresses
When the sorting system cannot resolve an address, the parcel gets status code AD-110 (address incomplete) and is held at the depot for 5 business days. If no correction arrives in that time, it is returned to the sender.
