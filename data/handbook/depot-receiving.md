# Depot drop-off and receiving

## Depot opening hours
Depots accept drop-offs from 07:00 to 20:00 on business days. Parcels dropped off before 18:30 leave on the same evening's linehaul. Later drop-offs leave the next evening.

## Bulk drop-offs
A drop-off of more than 50 parcels needs a manifest, uploaded in the portal at least 2 hours before arrival. Without a manifest the parcels are accepted but processed the next day.

## Pallets
Pallets may be up to 1.8 m high and weigh up to 800 kg. Parcels on a pallet must each carry their own label, facing outwards. Wrapped pallets with hidden labels are broken down at the sender's cost.
